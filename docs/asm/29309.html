727D: Tune data channel 1
Byte Purpose
$00 $00 for tone/noise else mute
$01 LSB of ...
$02 MSB of ...
$03 Frequency LSB
$04 Bits 4-7 attenuation, bits 0-3 frequency MSB or noise value
$05 Countdown until next note
$06 Attenuation. Initialized to $50
$07 Countdown for when to increase attenuation
$08 Speed. Initialized to $05
$09 Countdown for relative loops
