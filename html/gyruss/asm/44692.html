AE94: Upload sprite patterns
Fill VDP RAM from $2100 to $3868 with sprite patterns that may be flipped and shifted. One new pattern is generated with each call. Builds a table of VDP addresses in sprite_pattern_vdp_addrs_table. Used by the routine at start_screen.

AE97 CP $FF If maxed out
