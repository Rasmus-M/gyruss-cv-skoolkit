AFAE: Flip vertical
Take 8 bytes pointed to by $72DE and place them after in reverse order. Returns address of reversed bytes in $72DE. Used by the routine at upload_sprite_patterns.
flip_vert AFAE LD DE,(L72DE)
AFB2 LD HL,$0010
AFB5 ADD HL,DE
AFB6 LD B,$08
flip_vert_0 AFB8 LD A,(DE)
AFB9 DEC HL
AFBA LD (HL),A
AFBB INC DE
AFBC DJNZ flip_vert_0
AFBE LD (L72DE),HL
AFC1 RET
